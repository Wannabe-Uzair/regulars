05 — Voice
  • KEEP NEW MEMBERS PAST DAY 90. — tagline
  • 6 IN 10 NEW MEMBERS QUIT WITHIN THREE MONTHS. — problem
  • MEMBERS WHO STAY HIT 3 VISITS A WEEK. — the line
  • TRACK. FLAG. NUDGE. — how it works
  • FREE 90-DAY PILOT. THEN $99 A MONTH. — price
  • Calm, exact, sure. The type is loud; the words are not.
  • Numerals always: 6 in 10, 3 visits, day 90, $99.
  • State, don't shout. No hype, no exclamation marks or emojis.
  • Every statistic carries its source in small type.
  • No QR codes, contact details, launch dates or roles. Display only.
