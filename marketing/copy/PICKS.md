# Tenure copy round 1: pick the big lines

Voice: calm, exact, sure (brand/DESIGN.md §2). Each item has 2–3 options; **★** marks the recommendation. *Asterisks* mark yellow words. Option A is the current line where it already fits the new voice. Same content as `picks.json`.

## Brand lines

### Tagline `tagline`

- **Where:** slides/slides.html slide 1 "Tagline"; reel/reel.html end card #tag; app COPY login.tagline; slides/notes.md slide 1
- **Limits:** 1 line, ≤ 34 characters, caps (Anton h2 on slide 1 and the reel end card)

| | Option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ★ | GET EVERY NEW MEMBER TO *DAY 90.* | Already calm and concrete: one instruction, one number, the owner's goal. |
| B | KEEP NEW MEMBERS PAST *DAY 90.* | Names the outcome (members kept) instead of the task; four words shorter. |
| C | *MEMBERS WHO STAY.* | The quietest choice: one brand line on every piece; the hook still carries day 90. Slide 1 and slide 8 would then say the same line. |

### Sign-off under the logo `signoff`

- **Where:** slides/slides.html slide 8 "Sign-off"; slides/notes.md slide 8
- **Limits:** 1 line, ≤ 18 characters, caps, all yellow (Anton h1)

| | Option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ★ | *MEMBERS WHO STAY.* | Understated, outcome-first, already the brand's close. |
| B | *THE ONES WHO STAY.* | Echoes the line headline word for word, so the close answers slide 3. |

### Logo descriptor `descriptor`

- **Where:** brand/kit.js logo (outlined in brand/wordmark.js: rerun tools/make-assets.mjs); brand/assets/lockup-*.svg; brochure covers; standee; slide 1; reel logo scene and end card; app COPY brand.descriptor
- **Limits:** 1 line, 33–37 characters, caps. It is fitted to the word's width: longer sets below the standee's 48 pt floor, shorter makes every lockup taller

| | Option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ★ | MEMBER RETENTION FOR INDEPENDENT GYMS | Tells a passer-by the category and the audience in one line; the category name is the word owners search for. |
| B | THE FIRST 90 DAYS · INDEPENDENT GYMS | Concrete instead of category: says what Tenure watches, at the same size. |

### Hook under DAY 90 `hook`

- **Where:** brochure/brochure.html front cover (under the DAY / 90 hero); standee/standee.html under DAY 90
- **Limits:** 2 lines, ≤ 14 characters each and no wider than MEMBER THERE. as set on the standee (Anton h1 230 pt fills the width); DAY 90 hero unchanged, *90* yellow

| | Option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A ★ | GET EVERY NEW / MEMBER THERE. | A goal the owner sets, not a promise Tenure makes; reads aloud as one calm sentence. |
| B | SEE EVERY NEW / MEMBER THERE. | Softer, a coach walking someone in; but "see" can read as a promise that every member arrives. |
| C | THE DAY THAT / COUNTS. | The most understated, and shorter; drops the owner's task and the word members. |

### Starter template 3 `tpl-3`

- **Where:** app/server/seed/northside.mjs TEMPLATES[2]
- **Limits:** Name ≤ 20 characters; body ≤ 160 characters with variables filled; keep one template using {days}; GSM-7 characters only (no em dashes or curly quotes), so it sends as one text

| | Option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Back on track — Hey {first}, it's been {days} days. One visit this week gets you back on track. See you soon? - {coach} | Direct, but counting days at someone reads as pressure, and "gets you back on track" is a promise. |
